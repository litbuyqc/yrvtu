03 / Oopbuy shopping guide

A shortlist is only as good as its details.

Use a repeatable comparison process for every find. A clear record makes it easier to spot missing information and avoid comparing items with different specifications.

Discover your next category ↗

  1. Define the item you actually want

    Record its category, intended use, preferred material, color and fit. Compare similar specifications rather than relying on a shared brand name.

  2. Read the listing behind the image

    Check the seller, product description, selected variant and available photos. Note which details are confirmed and which still need clarification.

  3. Measure before choosing a size

    Use the specific seller's size chart. Compare clothing measurements with a similar garment you own; for footwear, follow the listing's measurement method.

  4. Estimate the complete cost

    Separate item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and applicable import charges. Recheck estimates when parcel and destination details are known.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a shopping research list used to organize product links and comparison notes. Useful fields include category, seller, price, size information, photo references and the date each detail was checked.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

This page provides a browsing guide and comparison checklist rather than a downloadable spreadsheet. Its shopping links open CAFANS or the specified CAFANS categories.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

Compare the specific product listing, material description, available measurements, size chart and photos. For shoes, review the seller's sizing guidance and available foot-length or insole information. For clothing, compare garment measurements with a similar item you own.

Does a product photo prove authenticity?

No. Photos and brand names alone do not establish authenticity or seller authorization. Check provenance and seller documentation, and use the brand's authorized sales channels when authenticity is essential.

What costs belong in a shopping comparison?

Record the item price, domestic shipping, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Some amounts depend on the destination, parcel weight and shipping method, so treat early totals as estimates.

Is this an official Oopbuy or fashion-brand website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide directing visitors to CAFANS. It is not an official website of Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès, and it does not claim endorsement by those businesses.
